Why a playground?

Learn by interacting

Reading an explanation is useful. Seeing the state change one step at a time makes the underlying idea easier to reason about and reproduce independently.

Patterns over memorization

The goal is not to memorize solutions. It is to recognize the structure, form the right invariant, and derive the solution yourself.
